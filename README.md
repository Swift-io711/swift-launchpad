# Swift Launchpad

Role & Goal: Create a high-converting, professional landing page for Swift.io, a digital agency specializing in building clean, modern, high-performance websites for freelancers and small business owners (auto-entrepreneurs). The main objective is to establish immediate trust, clarity, and authority at first glance.

Design & Atmosphere:

Style: Ultra-clean, modern, premium UI/UX. Deep navy/slate dark background with bright indigo or cyan accents, or a crisp white layout with dark slate typography and bold blue CTAs.

Typography: Clean sans-serif (Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans). High visual hierarchy with strong headlines.

Trust Signals: Clean grid, soft drop shadows, subtle glassmorphism cards, pixel-perfect alignment.

Page Structure:

Header:

Brand: "Swift.io" with a minimal geometric logo icon.

Navigation: "Services", "Réalisations", "Notre Méthode", "Tarifs", "FAQ".

Action CTA: "Demander un devis".

Hero Section:

Headline: "Votre site web professionnel, clé en main et conçu pour attirer vos clients."

Subheadline: "Nous créons des sites modernes, rapides et optimisés pour les auto-entrepreneurs et indépendants. Démarquez-vous sans vous soucier de la technique."

CTAs: Primary "Lancer mon projet" + Secondary "Découvrir nos créations".

Trust Badge Line: "✓ 100% Propriétaire • Livraison rapide • Support dédié".

Visual: Realistic high-res desktop & mobile mockup showcase of a modern website preview.

Value Proposition Grid (Why Trust Us):

4 distinct benefit cards:

Design Sur-Mesure (Visuels élégants adaptés à votre métier).

Optimisé SEO Local (Trouvable facilement sur Google).

Adapté Mobile & Rapide (Expérience utilisateur fluide sur smartphone).

Zéro Prise de Tête (On s'occupe de l'hébergement et du technique).

Portfolio Showcase:

Grid displaying 3 clean website previews for different freelance sectors (Artisan/BTP, Prestataire de services, Consultant).

Simple 3-Step Process:

Step 1: Echange initial & besoins.

Step 2: Design & intégration rapide.

Step 3: Validation & mise en ligne.

Contact & Lead Form:

Minimalist contact card: Name, Activity/Business, Email, Phone number, Project details.

Footer:

Swift.io branding, legal links, quick contacts.
